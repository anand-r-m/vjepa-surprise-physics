## 2. Units of analysis, ties
- Pairs as in the released code: video 1 pairs with the video that diverges latest from it (prefix-sharing); the other two pair together. 180 pairs, 90 quadruplet clusters (30 per block O1–O3). Prefix-sharing is verified for both pairs of every quadruplet. Sets: pairs for H1 and clean accuracy; possible videos (180) for H2/H3.
- **Ties.** For score type m (M1, M2, M0r, B1, B2, B3): τ_m = 2·10⁻⁴ × median over the 360 clean dev videos of the per-video score being compared, computed separately for each aggregation: mean over windows for H1/clean accuracy; for H2 (event-aligned max) and H3 (full-clip max) **one** τ_m = 2·10⁻⁴ × the median of the 360 clean **full-clip-max** scores, which is t*-free, so no τ depends on t*; computed from the production fp16 clean run (M1 mean ≈ 1.1·10⁻⁴). A pair verdict, a detection or an AUROC comparison is a tie iff |difference| ≤ τ_m; a tie counts 0.5 (tie-half is primary for every metric). Strict (`<`, the released convention) and exact-tie (τ = 0) versions are sensitivity analyses. τ_m is computed once, logged in DECISIONS, never changed. Ties are only evaluated between runs of identical precision and configuration. The same τ_m applies to every comparison for that score type and aggregation. A flip is any change among {impossible-higher, tie, possible-higher}. Disclosure: this moves already-seen B1/B2 clean tie-half numbers toward their strict values (conservative direction).

## 4. Event time and magnitude matching
For each possible video p and its prefix-sharing impossible partner v: D(t) = mean over pixels/channels of |v(t) − p(t)| after the model's resize, values in [0,1]. τ_D = max(2/255, 99.9th percentile of D over the first 10 frames of all dev pairs). **t*_on** = first native frame with D(t) > τ_D. **t*_off** (label-free): if after t*_on there is a run of ≥ 5 consecutive native frames with D ≤ τ_D before frame T, t*_off = first frame of the last such run; otherwise T. Pairs with t*_off < T are "transient" (count reported); abrupt edits switch on at t*_on and revert at t*_off. Uniform-middle-50% fallback only for pairs with no D > τ_D (count logged, expected 0). Pairs with t*_on > 94 (after the last window) are excluded from confirmatory H2 (count reported).
Matching per pair, outcome-blind (pixels only): m_i = mean_{t∈[t*_on,t*_off)} D_i(t) at M1 input resolution; m_ik(s) = the same quantity between edit_k(p_i; s) and ref_k(p_i); s*_ik solves m_ik(s) = m_i (closed form for lighting up to clipping; bisection for jolt/zoom, sub-pixel allowed with identical resampling for ref). Strengths: mid 1×, low 0.5×, high 2×. Achieved ratio r_ik reported per type (median, IQR, ECDF), also at M2's input resolution. Target: median r ∈ [0.95, 1.05] and ≥ 90% of pairs in [0.9, 1.1]. Infeasible pairs take the clipped strength and remain in the primary analysis. **Match failure:** if a type misses the target it stays in A, is flagged "match target missed" in every table, and A excluding that type is a sensitivity analysis; the primary analysis does not change. Descriptors reported: whole-frame L1 (matched), affected-area fraction, onset frame-difference at t*_on, M1 target-encoder feature distance. (Q3 answered YES; the "no" branch is retired.) If it had been "no": one Debug-calibrated strength per type (median match) is used, H2 is labelled "nominally magnitude-matched", and the abstract says so.
Window mapping: window w spans native frames 4w … 4w+30.

## 6. Primary analyses
**H1 (invariance).** Per-pair score y ∈ {0, 0.5, 1} (tie-half). For seeded edits y and flip are averaged over the 3 seeds at pair level. Δacc_e = mean_i(y_e,i − y_0,i). **Confirmatory Δacc p-value (one, fixed):** the two-sided scene-cluster-bootstrap p, p = min(1, 2·min(P*[Δacc* ≥ 0], P*[Δacc* ≤ 0])) over 10,000 quadruplet resamples stratified by block (seed 20261015; `p_dacc_cluster` in `src/vjepa_study/analysis/prereg.py`). Holm-14 is applied to these 7 Δacc p-values together with the 7 flip-vs-floor one-sided cluster-bootstrap p-values (p = P*[(flip_e − f0)* ≤ 0]) on the same draws; a Δacc test is "Holm-significant" iff its Holm-adjusted p < .05. The exact two-sided sign test on pairs with y_e,i ≠ y_0,i is reported alongside (and its own Holm-14 family, sign + flip) but never governs. Noise floor f0 = mean dither flip rate (under crop exclusion, f0 is computed on the same retained pairs). Family: Holm over 14 p-values per model (7 edits at high strength × {Δacc test, flip-vs-floor test}), flip_e − f0 on the same scene-cluster bootstrap draw (paired). **Violated** for edit e if [Δacc_e ≤ −5 pp and the cluster-bootstrap Δacc p is Holm-significant] or [flip_e ≥ 10% and flip_e − f0 ≥ 5 pp and the flip-vs-floor p is Holm-significant]. **Invariant** if the 95% lower bound of Δacc_e > −5 pp and the upper bound of (flip_e − f0) < 5 pp. Otherwise inconclusive. If f0 ≥ 10% (point estimate), that model's H1 is descriptive ("verdicts unstable under an invisible perturbation"). The cluster bootstrap always governs; disagreement with the sign test is counted and reported. Low/mid strengths are dose-response A3 (Holm over all 21, secondary). pp = absolute percentage points.
**H1 eligibility (fixed now).** A model is H1-confirmatory iff clean accuracy (mean aggregation, 180 pairs, ties at τ_m = 0.5) ≥ 80.0% (≥ 144 pair-credits) as a point estimate. **M1** is judged on the P2 fp32 clean run: 81.7% tie-half, 80.6% strict, eligible under either convention, so **M1 is H1-confirmatory**; never re-evaluated on the fp16 run, another τ or another aggregation. **M2** is judged on its fp16 clean run; number and status are logged in DECISIONS before any M2 edited-condition output is opened. Ineligible models are descriptive.

**H2 (specificity; headline).** Event-aligned max: for pair i, S is the max over windows w with 4w + 30 ≥ t*_on,i, using the same window set W_i for S(v_i), S(p_i), S(edit_k(p_i)), S(ref_k(p_i)) and B1/B2/B3. AUROC_m = P(Δ_viol − Δ_abrupt > τ_m) + 0.5·P(|Δ_viol − Δ_abrupt| ≤ τ_m) (a difference inside τ_m counts 0.5 whatever its sign; no double counting), over 180 Δ_viol versus all Δ_abrupt at mid strength, mean of per-type AUROCs, per seed then averaged. Δ_viol,i = S(v_i) − S(p_i); Δ_abrupt,ik = S(edit_k(p_i)) − S(ref_k(p_i)). Secondary: foil accuracy mean_i 1[S(v_i) > S(edit_k(p_i))]. Bootstrap: quadruplets resampled with replacement stratified by block (30 per block), all models on the same draw, percentile 95% CI. A = seed-mean pooled AUROC_M1. **D_k = A_M1 − A_Bk for k ∈ {1,2}; both are confirmatory specificity comparisons, Holm-corrected across the two (Holm-2, per model; replaces the earlier intersection–union rule).** Paired bootstrap 95% percentile CIs are reported for both D_k on the common draws. The p-value that feeds Holm is the one-sided paired-bootstrap p_k = P*[D_k* ≤ 0] over the 10,000 stratified quadruplet resamples (seed 20261015; p floored at 1/10,001 before entering Holm, which has no effect on any decision at α = .05). Holm-2: sort p_(1) ≤ p_(2); adjusted p_(1) = min(1, 2p_(1)), p_(2) = min(1, max(adj_(1), p_(2))). **Decision rule:** D_k is "significant" iff its Holm-adjusted p < .05; every table condition "D significant" in the outcome table below means that both D_1 and D_2 are significant (the table still requires beating both baselines, but each is now tested under a family-wise α = .05 over the two rather than by two unadjusted CIs; the CI lower bounds are descriptive and agree with the p-values only approximately). If only one of the two is significant the row is "Separable but not beyond low-level baseline" and the paper names the baseline that is not beaten. M1 − B3 secondary. **B1 ≡ `b1_windows` (frame difference over the predicted window; clean mean-aggregation 57.2% strict / 58.1% tie-half) and B2 ≡ `b2_windows` (copy-last-context-frame), for D_1, D_2 and every confirmatory use; `b1_windows` is the same array the spec's literal B2 wording describes, so there is no separate descriptive-only variant of it. No variant is substituted after data are seen.** T1 reports the paired clean difference M1 − B2 and states that copy-last-frame reaches 76.1% strict on clean dev pairs.

## 9. Power and limitations (stated before data)
With ~19% of pairs wrong at clean, edits can also fix pairs; the exact sign test (which does not govern; the cluster bootstrap does, §6) would reliably detect Δacc drops of about 10 pp or more (≈ 0.08 power for a 5 pp drop at Holm step 1 if 20% of wrong pairs flip to right); these sign-test figures are an approximate guide only and the cluster-bootstrap test's power was not computed; the Δacc arm cannot establish invariance to 5 pp. H2 now has two confirmatory comparisons (D_1, D_2) under Holm-2: the smaller p must be < .025 and the larger < .05, so the specificity claim is slightly EASIER to reach than under the earlier rule (both 95% CI lower bounds > 0, about both one-sided p < .025): the smaller p faces the same .025 threshold and the larger faces .05 instead of .025 (never harder; disclosed here as a change in stringency); no power computation was made for D_k. H2's 0.80 threshold sits near the ceiling for a perfectly specific M1 under mean aggregation (≈ 0.80), which is why the event-aligned aggregation and the detection-limited rule are fixed. Every headline is stratified by visible/occluded (proxy: abrupt onset if D(t*_on) ≥ 0.5 · mean D over the event window) and by block, descriptive only. Discussion does not say "physics-specific" without "relative to these abrupt-event types"; event-type frequency in pretraining is an alternative explanation. Scenes may share textures; background-cluster bootstrap is exploratory if IDs are recoverable.
